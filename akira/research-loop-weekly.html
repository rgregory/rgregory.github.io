Canonical loop: 50-research/benford-financial-fraud-validation/

Accomplishments since prior report

Evidence-backed findings

Benford's Law is best treated as a screening heuristic, not a standalone fraud detector. It can flag datasets whose leading-digit distribution is surprising under Benford assumptions, but it only applies to certain kinds of naturally occurring, multi-scale numerical data. Standard statistical validation could catch some manipulations if the data are appropriate, the sample is large enough, and the manipulation changes digit frequencies enough; it would not reliably catch fraud that preserves leading-digit structure, affects only small samples, or occurs in data that should not follow Benford in the first place.

How do biofilms regulate?

Scaffolded
Canonical loop: 50-research/biofilm-regulation/

Accomplishments since prior report

Evidence-backed findings

Biofilms regulate through layered feedback: cells sense density and environment, switch gene-expression programs, produce or remodel extracellular matrix, specialize across chemical gradients, and eventually disperse when conditions favor movement. Regulation is not only a single molecular switch; it is a multi-scale control system spanning quorum sensing, second messengers such as cyclic-di-GMP, stress responses, matrix mechanics, nutrient/oxygen gradients, and community composition.

godel-importance

Why is Gödel's work important?

Advanced
Canonical loop: 50-research/godel-importance/

Accomplishments since prior report

sources.md (2026-07-18), loop.md (2026-07-18), findings.md (2026-07-18)

Evidence-backed findings

Gödel's work is important because it showed that formal mathematical systems have intrinsic limits: any sufficiently expressive, consistent formal system cannot prove every truth expressible in its own language, and cannot prove its own consistency by its own accepted methods. That result reshaped the foundations of mathematics, clarified the limits of Hilbert-style formalism, helped define the modern boundary between proof, truth, computation, and mechanism, and became a reference point for later debates in philosophy of mathematics and philosophy of mind.
