Hypotheses

  • The recurring friction is orchestration provenance rather than tone: unpinned jobs and mixed generated state blur whether work ran, succeeded, or was served.

Cross-thread pattern

Akira is becoming more reliable when activity becomes typed, reviewable state: research becomes source manifests, civic discovery becomes automation boundaries, interaction improvement becomes outcome records rather than tone guesses, and publishing becomes a stable artifact plus independent live verification.
