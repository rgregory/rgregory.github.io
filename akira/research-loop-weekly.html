Shared decision

Prototype grant-accountable completion in one workflow

Do not begin with a platform or another taxonomy. Start with one consequential workflow and make its effect, issuer, accountable principal, permitted evidence, tool scope, expiration, verifier result, resource ceiling, residual state, and rollback path both machine- and human-readable.

The prototype passes only when an independent reviewer can reject the action or closeout using that record.
