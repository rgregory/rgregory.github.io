# Operating Principles

## Stable public publication
Public-facing recurring reports need one canonical filename, an index link, and live verification. A push is not success until the served page matches the source artifact.

Sources: [[2026-07-24-research-loop-weekly-publication]] [[2026-07-24-weekly-sitdown-provenance]]

## Separate run states
Execution, artifact generation, publication, and live verification are distinct states. Treat any one of them as partial evidence only.

Sources: [[2026-07-24-weekly-sitdown-provenance]] [[2026-07-24-civic-service-discovery]]

## Preserve service-family boundaries
When a civic workflow spans multiple services, model the families separately. If payment or contact is required, stop at a reviewable prep artifact rather than pretending automation is complete.

Source: [[2026-07-24-civic-service-discovery]]

## Evidence thresholds for sensory-wellness claims
For sensory-wellness claims, separate direct exposure effects from category membership and subjective comfort. Require loudness-matched direct exposure and objective physiology before promoting a causal claim.

Sources: [[2026-07-26-rain-sound-evidence-threshold]] [[2026-07-27 — rain-sound research loop]]

## Agent research is a lifecycle problem
Evaluate agent improvements as task-shaped lifecycle changes: state transitions, evidence validity, authority boundaries, and persistent failures matter more than aggregate benchmark aura.

Sources: [[2026-07-27 — AI Agents research loop]] [[2026-07-28-ai-agents-research-loop]]
