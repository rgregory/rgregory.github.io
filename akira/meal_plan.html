Meal breakdown

Monday, 2026-07-27

Lunch — Halloumi Couscous Roasted Veg Boxes (23g protein)

Pearl couscous, roasted vegetables, and halloumi that stays satisfying even at room temp.

Portable: yes · Microwave friendly: yes · Prep: 26 minutes

Ingredients:

  • zucchini (2)
  • bell peppers (2)
  • cherry tomatoes (1 pint)
  • halloumi (8 oz)
  • pearl couscous (1 cup dry)
  • olive oil (2 tbsp)
  • oregano (1 tsp)

Prep note: Roast the vegetables and sear the halloumi in the same pan to reduce cleanup.

Dinner — Spinach Ricotta White Bean Baked Orzo (22g protein)

Orzo baked with ricotta, white beans, spinach, and marinara in one skillet.

One-pan preferred: yes · Microwave friendly: yes · Prep: 35 minutes

Ingredients:

  • baby spinach (5 oz)
  • ricotta (1 cup)
  • parmesan (1/2 cup)
  • white beans (2 cans)
  • orzo (1 cup dry)
  • marinara (2 cups)
  • vegetable broth (2 cups)

Prep note: Stir in spinach at the end so it keeps some texture.

Tuesday, 2026-07-28

Lunch — Tofu Peanut Noodle Jars (22g protein)

Rice noodles, baked tofu, shredded vegetables, and peanut sauce in grab-and-go jars.

Portable: yes · Microwave friendly: yes · Prep: 25 minutes

Ingredients:

  • red cabbage (1/2 head)
  • carrots (3)
  • cilantro (1 bunch)
  • lime (2)
  • extra-firm tofu (14 oz)
  • rice noodles (8 oz)
  • peanut butter (1/3 cup)
  • soy sauce (3 tbsp)
  • maple syrup (1 tbsp)

Prep note: Layer sauce at the bottom and noodles on top so the vegetables stay crisp.

Dinner — One-Pan Pesto Tortellini with Peas and Cannellini (21g protein)

Cheese tortellini, peas, cannellini beans, and pesto in a creamy green skillet dinner.

One-pan preferred: yes · Microwave friendly: yes · Prep: 22 minutes

Ingredients:

  • baby spinach (5 oz)
  • cheese tortellini (20 oz)
  • parmesan (1/2 cup)
  • peas (10 oz)
  • cannellini beans (2 cans)
  • basil pesto (1/2 cup)
  • vegetable broth (1 cup)

Prep note: Use a splash of pasta water or broth to loosen the sauce if needed.

Wednesday, 2026-07-29

Lunch — Egg and White Bean Pasta Salad (21g protein)

Short pasta tossed with hard-boiled eggs, white beans, spinach, and a lemony dressing.

Portable: yes · Microwave friendly: no · Prep: 22 minutes

Ingredients:

  • baby spinach (5 oz)
  • celery (3 stalks)
  • dill (1 bunch)
  • eggs (6)
  • white beans (2 cans)
  • whole-wheat rotini (12 oz)
  • olive oil (3 tbsp)
  • lemon (1)
  • Dijon mustard (1 tbsp)

Prep note: Use extra dressing so the pasta stays moist after chilling.

Dinner — Sheet-Pan Gnocchi with Chickpeas and Mozzarella (20g protein)

A one-pan dinner with shelf-stable gnocchi, chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, and mozzarella.

One-pan preferred: yes · Microwave friendly: yes · Prep: 30 minutes

Ingredients:

  • cherry tomatoes (1 pint)
  • baby spinach (5 oz)
  • fresh mozzarella (8 oz)
  • shelf-stable gnocchi (18 oz)
  • chickpeas (2 cans)
  • olive oil (2 tbsp)
  • Italian seasoning (1 tsp)

Prep note: Everything roasts together; add mozzarella in the last five minutes.

Thursday, 2026-07-30

Lunch — Lentil Feta Farro Salad (20g protein)

Farro, lentils, roasted peppers, feta, and arugula with red-wine vinaigrette.

Portable: yes · Microwave friendly: no · Prep: 24 minutes

Ingredients:

  • arugula (5 oz)
  • roasted red peppers (1 jar)
  • shallot (1)
  • feta (5 oz)
  • farro (1 cup dry)
  • cooked lentils (2 cups)
  • olive oil (3 tbsp)
  • red wine vinegar (2 tbsp)

Prep note: Pack arugula on top so it stays fresh until lunch.

Dinner — Harissa Chickpea Halloumi Tray Bake (20g protein)

A sheet-pan dinner with chickpeas, carrots, red onion, and halloumi under a mild harissa glaze.

One-pan preferred: yes · Microwave friendly: yes · Prep: 30 minutes

Ingredients:

  • carrots (4)
  • red onion (1)
  • parsley (1 bunch)
  • halloumi (8 oz)
  • chickpeas (2 cans)
  • harissa (2 tbsp)
  • olive oil (2 tbsp)

Prep note: Use a mild harissa so the leftovers stay approachable and not too intense.

Friday, 2026-07-31

Lunch — Edamame Brown Rice Crunch Boxes (19g protein)

Brown rice, edamame, cucumbers, peanuts, and sesame dressing in bento-style boxes.

Portable: yes · Microwave friendly: yes · Prep: 18 minutes

Ingredients:

  • cucumber (1)
  • radishes (1 bunch)
  • scallions (1 bunch)
  • shelled edamame (16 oz)
  • brown rice (1 cup dry)
  • peanuts (3/4 cup)
  • sesame oil (1 tbsp)
  • soy sauce (2 tbsp)
  • rice vinegar (2 tbsp)

Prep note: These are fine cold but also reheat well if you want the rice warm.

Dinner — Sesame Egg Fried Rice with Edamame (19g protein)

A fast skillet dinner with eggs, edamame, rice, and plenty of crunchy vegetables.

One-pan preferred: yes · Microwave friendly: yes · Prep: 20 minutes

Ingredients:

  • carrots (2)
  • scallions (1 bunch)
  • eggs (6)
  • shelled edamame (12 oz)
  • cooked rice (4 cups)
  • sesame oil (1 tbsp)
  • soy sauce (3 tbsp)

Prep note: Great for using leftover rice and any extra scallions or carrots from lunches.

Sunday prep / shared components

  • Shared ingredients to batch once: cherry tomatoes, halloumi, olive oil, carrots, soy sauce, baby spinach, eggs, white beans.
  • Cook one base grain on Sunday if the plan includes quinoa, farro, couscous, or rice so lunches assemble quickly.
  • Wash and chop crunchy vegetables in advance; keep dressings and nuts separate for work lunches.
  • Press tofu, roast vegetables, and boil eggs in the same prep block to keep the weeknight dinners easy.

Rotation notes

  • Lunches remain portable and low-mess for work; dinners stay weeknight-easy.
