2) Learned

  • Agent safety is not one initial permission check. Delegation can lose identity or blend context; grants, capability claims, and recovery rules need re-checkable boundaries.
  • Sandboxing is both a security control and a cost/capacity control. Fan-out should carry explicit resource ceilings.
  • Generic AI-readiness assessments are crowded. The sharper offer is insurer-, regulator-, board-, and customer-ready evidence that an adoption program is governed and recoverable.
  • For Parkinson’s, “levodopa stops working after X years” is too crude; disease progression, dose exposure, phenotype, and patient factors interact, while SB-0107 is not yet a human treatment option.
  • Monitoring quality depends on showing its limits: eBay search bootstrapping and Eggleston access were blocked; mileage is absent on the two local classified candidates.

Cross-thread pattern

The same design principle now connects agent research, AI-risk services, cyber response, and vault operations: systems become credible when they preserve evidence and authority across the whole path from a request to an outcome.

That makes “governed execution” a plausible shared architecture rather than a research slogan.
