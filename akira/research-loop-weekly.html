Cross-loop reading

Boundary-complete execution

Decision: Use boundary-complete execution as the shared design test. A high-impact action should retain accountable identity, context and provenance, policy, grant evidence, delegation-chain controls, resource ceilings, and completion proof from admission through closeout.

Agent research supplies the lifecycle model. Provenance-first memory supplies the durable record. Cyber work supplies the control/evidence vocabulary. Philosophy supplies the discipline of not promoting fluent but under-challenged synthesis. The next artifact should be a small admission-and-closeout contract, not another broad taxonomy.
