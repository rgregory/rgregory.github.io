Practice Lead Lens: AI Risk, Insurance, and Market Alignment

How Insurance Agencies / Carriers Are Handling AI Risk

  • Treating AI as a governed business process, not just a technology tool: written AI-system programs, accountable owners, inventories, data controls, validation, monitoring, and audit evidence.
  • Moving AI into underwriting and claims risk conversations: agencies and carriers are watching model drift, unfair discrimination, data provenance, third-party AI, and aggregation/concentration risk.
  • Asking for evidence: AI acceptable-use rules, vendor attestations, model inventories, data-flow controls, incident playbooks, and documented human oversight are becoming the saleable artifacts.
  • Practical opening: position cybersecurity/risk work as a bridge between AI governance, cyber hygiene, privacy, vendor risk, and cyber-insurance readiness.
  • Regulatory baseline: NAIC Model Bulletin on the Use of AI Systems by Insurers — https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/artificial-intelligence
